### Student Answer (Handwritten)
![Student handwritten answer on osmosis](sample-answer.jpg)

**Transcribed answer:**
> Osmosis is the movement of solute molecules through a selectively permeable membrane. The solvent moves from an area where there is low solute to an area where there is more solute.
